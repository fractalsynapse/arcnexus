# Maintain ArcNexus guidance

Apply [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), the shared
[skill-writing procedure](../writing-skills/SKILL.md).

Keep inherited skills byte-for-byte aligned with the recorded Scaffold revision.
Put ArcNexus procedures in project-owned skills and route readers to the canonical
charter, policy and contracts. Review guidance changes against captured ownership
and authority. Integrated guidance applies to future assignments. The current
assignment keeps its captured permissions, operations and acceptance.

Use the [Agent contract proposal](../../../packages/agent/contract.md) for the
central actor model. Route activity, capability and context guidance through that
boundary and the component's explicit activity mappings. Use the
[Team proposal](../../../packages/team/contract.md) when routing steward discovery
and ownership. Keep the application's transport behavior at its adapter boundary.

Check metadata and declared references on the tracked candidate, then review Markdown links and walk through
the decisions affected by the change. Verify that a new instruction cannot expand
captured authority, a repeat supplier request keeps the existing accountable
assignment, and pending required CI leaves the candidate awaiting integration.

[Manager](../../../packages/manager/contract.md) owns directives and skills.
Route guidance input changes there, and route capture or catalog validation changes
to Architecture. Report observed results and unresolved requests to the
caller for delivery.

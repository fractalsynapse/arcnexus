# Enroll and reconcile trusted execution capacity

Let an authorized operator obtain a verified launcher build for an available target, exchange a single-use registration code and explicitly admit or decline the pending provider. Show authenticated online, offline and pending states with text labels.

Admission grants scoped reconnect credentials. Revocation invalidates credentials, disconnects the provider and removes it from new launch routing while retaining execution evidence.

Resolve assignments to verified projects, approved environments and directory bindings. Reconcile existing containers before repeating launch effects. Keep durable state and provider credentials on the execution machine under their established isolation rules.

## Observable success

- Available Linux x64 and ARM64 downloads have matching published checksums; unavailable builds remain explicit.
- Codes cannot be reused and pending providers cannot execute admitted work.
- Reconnect during launch reuses the matching container.
- Revocation prevents new assignments and preserves interrupted-work evidence.
- External publication and real-project activation remain subject to qualification.

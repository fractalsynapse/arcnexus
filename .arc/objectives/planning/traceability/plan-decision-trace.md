# Trace hosted Plan decisions from objective review to goal alignment

An operator reviewing hosted Plan can follow a durable, readable chain from the original objective and complete clarification dialogue through the exact reviewed proposal, human decision and resulting goal alignment. Preserve objective identity and explicit refinement history so later assessments can explain why accepted direction changed.

Keep the selected project, Plan execution and captured guide revision consistent across objective review, proposal details, approval and alignment views. A newer execution or refreshed project revision must not silently replace the context being reviewed.

Make each objective's relationship to existing or proposed goals explicit, including retained, changed or removed goals and unresolved mappings. Keep source alignment separate from observed implementation support. Preserve pending, declined and justified no-change outcomes distinctly; none should imply approval or delivery of proposed records.

Retain the exact proposal, relevant baseline, question and complete human answer through reconnect and restart. Approval applies only to the reviewed guide bytes and baseline. Changed content or a changed relevant baseline requires a reconciled, reviewable decision. Link accepted records and their delivery disposition back to that decision through the existing journal and application lifecycle.

## Observable success

- An operator can navigate from an objective to its complete clarification, exact proposed diff, recorded decision and resulting goal dispositions without reconstructing a conversation or inferring relationships from timestamps.
- Reconnect and host-restart scenarios preserve the same pending proposal and answer identity. A newer Plan execution does not change the selected review context or cause duplicate questions or answers.
- Approved, declined, unanswered, unresolved and no-change cases remain distinguishable. Declining proposed edits preserves the original objective, while an unanswered proposal remains pending.
- A changed proposal or relevant baseline cannot reuse approval for different guide bytes. The reconciled proposal retains its relationship to the original objective and prior decision.
- Goal alignment identifies its supporting captured sources independently of execution evidence. Accepted records remain traceable to the exact human decision and actual delivery disposition, with observations kept outside standing goal aspirations.

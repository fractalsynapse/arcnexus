# Connect approved targets to strategies and verified progress

Preserve the proposed governance outcome for exact architecture review before runtime adoption. Quantitative goals identify the measure, unit, target, calculation and comparable conditions. Changing success or its target requires the existing goal approval path.

Link strategy revisions and milestone tactics to approved goal revisions and intended measures. Keep assignments and observed values in run records. Retain measure definitions, versions, inputs, candidate or staging identity and supporting observations.

Show baseline and latest verified progress separately from candidate measurements and individual delivery verdicts. A verified improvement may leave the standing target unmet. Measurement-definition changes require a comparable baseline before interpreting trends. Illustrative mockup values do not become project targets.

## Observable success

- Missing evidence remains unavailable rather than zero.
- Candidate observations do not replace the last verified staging value.
- Later runs recover strategy and measure history and can justify continuation, revision or no change.
- Only an approved goal refinement changes the success target.

# Provide complete guidance that supports useful decisions

Give each activation complete selected purpose, role, procedure and standards, with source boundaries separating operating guidance, task input and observations. Make the originating finding, current checks, acknowledged receipts and complete retained answers discoverable.

Keep other responsibilities discoverable through a source-linked component index. Preserve complete reports and artifacts through existing reads and indexes without recursive copying, arbitrary truncation or another evidence store. Instructions must describe actual available capabilities and expose missing mappings honestly.

Evaluate guidance by decisions and useful delivery as well as static validity. Realistic public fixtures must distinguish the required behavior from plausible shortcuts. Human approval of prose alone does not demonstrate improved execution.

## Observable success

- The real harness receives complete selected guidance with captured source identity.
- Nested repairs retain the originating finding and complete relevant answers.
- Missing operations remain visible as capability gaps.
- Live evaluations connect changed guidance to observed decisions and delivery, retaining uncertainty about causality.

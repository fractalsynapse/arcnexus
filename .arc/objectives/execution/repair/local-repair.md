# Repair ordinary failures at the responsible activity

Return invalid responses, refused operations and ordinary technical failures to their responsible caller with exact diagnostics. Preserve acknowledged edits, complete answers, original acceptance and unaffected evidence.

Repair failures within the accepted milestone at the earliest applicable activity that can correct them. Refresh affected downstream checks and review. Routine repair does not require a new human decision or plan. Changes in owner, scope or sequencing and final independent rejection use the established escalation path.

Stop unchanged repetition when the same finding and relevant inputs admit no progress. Retain an actionable hold or escalation. Newly completed clarification or research can change the relevant inputs; unrelated artifacts and duplicate answers cannot manufacture progress.

## Observable success

- Successive distinct failures receive local correction without unnecessary replanning.
- Self-review findings resume the appropriate earlier activity with the originating requirement intact.
- Restart preserves receipts, answers and exact diagnostics.
- Unchanged repetition stops explicitly, while genuine corrective progress continues.

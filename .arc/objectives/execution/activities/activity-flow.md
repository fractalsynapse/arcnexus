# Support explicit component activity flows

Preserve the proposed activity-flow outcome for exact contract review before adoption. Let engineering components declare an ordered sequence and activities available on explicit request, using existing activity identities. Missing activities remain unavailable and project-selected activities retain their established bindings.

Resolve order, instructions, executor, authority and required checks from captured architecture. Sequence order establishes dependencies within the milestone. Record inapplicability with reasons, and return requested work to its waiting activity.

Permit eligible same-owner repairs to resume an earlier applicable activity while preserving acceptance and unaffected evidence. Changes in owner, scope or sequencing and final goal-level rejection follow the established planning route. Host-only release authority remains protected.

## Observable success

- Different declared sequences run through the same lifecycle.
- Unknown IDs and repeated memberships are rejected.
- Requested work preserves the original position.
- Invalid rewind targets and attempts to activate host-only work are refused.
- Historical plans retain their original activity identities.

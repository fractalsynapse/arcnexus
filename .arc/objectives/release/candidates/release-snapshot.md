# Prepare production scope from a pinned verified staging snapshot

Preserve the proposed release-preparation outcome for exact contract review before adoption. Establish the prior production release's recorded source staging commit as the exclusive range boundary and a selected verified descendant as the inclusive boundary. Missing ancestry or required verification produces a named hold.

Derive complete included scope from staging history, retaining merge order, issue links, acceptance evidence and unresolved attribution. Include every change in the range, including tested merge resolutions.

Pin the source boundary, selected snapshot and derived scope when preparing a candidate. New staging observations may offer newer choices but cannot silently replace the prepared candidate. Explicit replacement creates a new identity and refreshes affected reviews and approval. Production authorization remains separate.

## Observable success

- Squash history uses the retained staging source rather than guessing from production ancestry.
- A pinned candidate remains stable when newer staging becomes eligible.
- Preview and explicit replacement expose the actual scope change.
- Unlinked commits and partially accepted work remain visible.

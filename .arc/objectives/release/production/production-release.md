# Authorize and verify exact production releases

Preserve the proposed production lifecycle for exact contract review. Existing maintainer authority remains in force until an approved amendment explicitly enables trusted-host effects.

Bind specialist reviews, required checks and the maintainer's decision to the exact candidate, production base, scope, tag and deliverables. Changed identities require revalidation and renewed approval. Holds and change requests preserve their candidate and reasoning.

After authorization, reconcile and record merge, tag and publication effects separately, preserving immutable identities. Verify actual published deliverables before claiming delivery. Pending or unavailable evidence remains a named hold.

A failed post-release check preserves the released artifacts and creates one attributable issue-linked incident with complete evidence. Retry reuses the same incident identity. Correction proceeds through staging and a fresh authorized production release.

## Observable success

- Specialist rejection blocks approval and changed candidates invalidate prior approval.
- Cancellation and uncertain effects resume from observed provider state without duplicate publication.
- Delivered status requires confirmed effects and passing required post-release checks.
- Failed checks retain the released state and one recoverable incident.

# Make activity verification declarative and truthful

Let activities select their required verification through validated operation bindings. Resolve declared defaults and explicit overrides consistently for direct invocation and automatic completion. Treat arguments as data and preserve host-owned identity, confinement and authority.

Require applicable completion checks before progression. An expected behavioral red identifies the exercised assertion and intended missing behavior; dependency, compiler and fixture failures do not establish it. Preserve explicit distinctions among passing, failing, skipped and inapplicable observations.

Reuse observations only when relevant inputs, parameters, bindings, runtime and environment match. Unknown applicability requires fresh evidence. Local claims cannot replace mandatory assembled verification.

## Observable success

- Different activities reuse one operation with distinct validated selectors and identical resolution across invocation paths.
- Invalid overrides and empty explicit selections produce precise diagnostics.
- Expected-red scenarios distinguish product behavior from setup failures.
- Changed parameters or relevant inputs prevent stale evidence reuse.

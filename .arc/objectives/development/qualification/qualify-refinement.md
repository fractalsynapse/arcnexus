# Qualify useful continuous self-development

Demonstrate continuous self-development through the production composition and real model. Each round assesses objectives, goals and architecture, then evaluates every captured approved goal in priority order. Preserve the round's captured inventory and completed outcomes across interruption; subsequent fresh rounds may consume newly accepted direction.

Qualify three consecutive clean real rounds that collectively demonstrate an approved guide improvement, useful staging delivery and later consumption of prior findings. Justified no-change assessments and handled local repairs remain valid outcomes. Operator state repair, bypassed checks and uncontrolled aborts break qualification; an execution-path correction starts a fresh streak.

Retain assessments, approvals, repair routes, runtime identities, checks and staging commits. Keep deterministic tests, native enforcement, hosted CI and live qualification distinguishable. Broader operator-facing activation remains subject to the qualification boundary and any exact authorized exception.

## Observable success

- Three consecutive production rounds exercise the complete loop and retain every captured goal's disposition.
- Approval pauses resume the same round without replaying completed work.
- Later assessments demonstrably use earlier findings.
- Qualification reports expose failures and assistance rather than counting them as clean completion.

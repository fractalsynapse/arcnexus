# Improve efficiency using attributable measurements

Expose elapsed work and usage during execution, with attributable breakdowns by round, phase, task, activity and actual execution profile. Preserve profile aliases, resolved models and settings. Distinguish wall time, nested durations and approval waits.

Keep provider accounting semantics intact. Cached or reasoning subsets must not be added twice; absent usage remains unknown. Cost estimates require a verified dated pricing or billing basis and remain distinct from billed amounts.

Link aggregates to retained observations and evaluate efficiency against useful outcomes, reliability and quality. Later planning and architecture assessments consume earlier findings and evaluate concrete improvement hypotheses. Measurements do not authorize quotas, automatic profile changes or weakened acceptance.

## Observable success

- Live totals reconcile with underlying records without duplicate token or duration accounting.
- Missing usage and unavailable pricing remain visibly unknown.
- Failed work, repairs, waits and successful delivery are represented.
- A later round demonstrably uses measurements to choose or evaluate an improvement.

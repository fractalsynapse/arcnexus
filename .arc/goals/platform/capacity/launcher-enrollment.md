# Enroll and reconcile trusted execution capacity

Let authorized operators obtain verified launcher builds for available targets, exchange single-use registration codes and explicitly admit or decline pending providers. Show authenticated online, offline and pending states with text labels.

Admission grants scoped reconnect credentials. Revocation invalidates credentials, disconnects the provider and removes new launch routing while retaining execution evidence.

Resolve assignments to verified projects, approved environments and directory bindings. Reconcile existing containers before repeating launch effects. Keep durable state and credentials on the execution machine under established isolation rules. External publication and real-project activation retain their qualification gates.

## Success measures

- Available Linux x64 and ARM64 downloads match published checksums; unavailable builds remain explicit.
- Codes cannot be reused and pending providers cannot execute admitted work.
- Reconnect during launch reuses the matching container.
- Revocation prevents new assignments and preserves interrupted-work evidence.

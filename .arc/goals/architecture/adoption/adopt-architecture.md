# Adopt approved architecture coherently within a run

Resolve one validated operating model with source provenance for guidance, ownership, operations, profiles and completion checks. Preserve explicit empty lists and approved replacement semantics; omission cannot silently delete records or expand authority.

After an exact approved update and required implementation validation, adopt the model at a recorded activity boundary. Complete or cancel an in-flight activation first. Regenerate the next activation's context, authority and checks together. Revalidate retained work, checkpoints and affected evidence while preserving original acceptance and historical authority. Candidate files cannot authorize execution.

## Success measures

- An approved implemented operation becomes available to the next eligible activity in the same run.
- Removals, changed ownership and empty lists agree across inspection and activation.
- Stale approval, unavailable handlers, incompatible ownership and interrupted adoption produce precise recoverable diagnostics without mixed model versions.
- Historical activations and original acceptance retain their identities.

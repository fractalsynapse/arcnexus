# Trace verified goal evidence to its exact observation

When a goal mapping cites a verified run, preserve the selected observation in
its readable report alongside the run identity and evaluated revision. Identify
the selected operation, independent review criterion, or delivery observation
explicitly. Keep source coverage distinct from observed results, and keep check,
review and delivery evidence distinguishable. Keep observed results in mapping
reports and standing goals independent of component ownership.

## Success measures

Focused tests exercise operation, review-criterion and delivery citations through
mapping validation and readable report generation. Each report identifies the
selected observation, run and evaluated revision without requiring inference
from the run's other observations. Source coverage alone cannot establish a
demonstrated rating, and unknown or stale verified citations remain rejected.

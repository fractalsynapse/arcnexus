# Recover steward holds after independent rejection

When a steward cannot complete a criterion after independent review, return the
exact diagnostic and retained check or checked-edit evidence to Direction. Keep
the same issue, secured checkpoint, recorded answers and original acceptance.
Sequence corrections through the ordinary task plan without widening authority or
marking an unmet criterion complete. Permit an oversized correction to become
smaller sequential runnable contributions, each traceable to the original
criterion and finding.

Continue when a repairable hold admits concrete correction work. If that correction
returns the same hold without observable progress, stop automatic repetition and
report the unresolved criterion, diagnostic, evidence and required next action.
A genuine human decision or external prerequisite stops dependent work at an
explicit gate naming the prerequisite. Preserve the task for continuation after
that prerequisite is satisfied. Healthy progress alone must not trigger a stop.

## Success measures

A scenario begins with an independent rejection and a subsequent steward hold.
Direction receives the exact diagnostic, check exit and log or checked-edit receipt,
and sequences a correction that makes progress and eventually satisfies the
original criterion through checks and independent review. The issue, checkpoint,
answers and original acceptance remain traceable throughout.

An oversized correction is split into sequential contributions that each execute
within captured authority and retain their evidence before the next contribution.
Their assembled candidate is assessed against the original acceptance.

A correction that repeats the same hold without progress produces an explicit
stop before another automatic repetition. Separate human-decision and external-
prerequisite scenarios name the required action, retain evidence and position,
and resume only after the prerequisite is observed as satisfied. No stopped or
held task is reported as accepted or delivered.

# Recover prepared work when staging moves

When staging moves after task-branch preparation, preserve the existing issue,
secured checked bytes, evidence and verbatim human answers. Retain the original
base, acceptance and review observations as history. Recover only against newly
captured ownership, staging base and applicable acceptance, preserving the original
requirements and obtaining architectural authority for any required boundary or
acceptance change. Refuse edits outside the new capture and retain conflicts for
resolution.

Treat approval for the earlier candidate or capture as inapplicable to the
recovered candidate. Obtain fresh required checks and independent review against
the recovered candidate and its captured acceptance before delivery. Keep delivery
pinned to the newly reviewed tree and staging parent; further staging movement
requires renewed reconciliation.

Observe GitHub issue-branch and pull-request state before retrying an uncertain
or outdated publication. Reconcile the retained delivery with those facts, reuse
its matching pull request or confirmed merge, and refuse conflicting identities.
Do not create a second delivery to escape uncertainty or stale approval. A genuine
human or external prerequisite produces an explicit gate with retained evidence.

## Success measures

A scenario secures a checked checkpoint, records a human answer, then moves staging
before continuation. Recovery preserves the issue, checkpoint bytes and exact
answer while capturing the new base, ownership and acceptance. Changed ownership
is enforced, and a conflicting checked change is retained and diagnosed before
any unauthorized overwrite.

Checks and independent review evaluate the recovered final candidate. Attempts to
publish or merge using approval for the earlier tree, base or capture are refused.
The successful path identifies the newly reviewed tree, staging parent, required
checks, independent verdict and confirmed staging merge.

Publication scenarios lose a provider response or retain an outdated open pull
request across staging movement. Recovery inspects provider facts before effects,
reconciles the existing publication with the newly accepted candidate, and yields
one task delivery and one pull request. A confirmed prior merge is reported without
a duplicate delivery; conflicting or unavailable provider facts prevent a success
claim and preserve the work and evidence for continuation.

# Repair ordinary failures at the responsible activity

Return invalid responses, refused operations and ordinary technical failures to their responsible caller with exact diagnostics. Preserve acknowledged edits, complete answers, original acceptance and unaffected evidence.

Repair within the accepted milestone at the earliest applicable activity that can correct the finding, then refresh affected downstream checks and review. Routine repair adds no human or planning gate. Changes in owner, scope or sequencing and final independent rejection use the established escalation path.

Stop unchanged repetition when the finding and relevant inputs admit no progress. Retain an actionable hold or escalation. Completed clarification or research can change relevant inputs; unrelated artifacts and duplicate answers cannot manufacture progress.

## Success measures

- Distinct failures receive local correction without unnecessary replanning.
- Self-review resumes the appropriate earlier activity with the originating requirement intact.
- Restart preserves receipts, complete answers and exact diagnostics.
- Unchanged repetition stops explicitly while genuine correction continues.

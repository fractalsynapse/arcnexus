# Support explicit component activity flows

Preserve the proposed activity-flow outcome for exact contract review before adoption. Allow declared ordered sequences and activities available on explicit request, using existing activity identities. Missing activities remain unavailable; project-selected activities retain their bindings.

Resolve order, instructions, executor, authority and required checks from captured architecture. Sequence establishes dependencies within the milestone. Record inapplicability with reasons and return requested work to its waiting activity.

Eligible same-owner repairs may resume an earlier applicable activity while preserving acceptance and unaffected evidence. Changes in owner, scope or sequencing and final goal-level rejection follow established planning routes. Preserve host-only release authority.

## Success measures

- Different declared sequences use the same lifecycle.
- Unknown IDs and repeated memberships are rejected.
- Requested work preserves the original position.
- Invalid rewinds and attempts to activate host-only work are refused.
- Historical plans retain their original activity identities.

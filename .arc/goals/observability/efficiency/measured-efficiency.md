# Improve efficiency using attributable measurements

Expose elapsed work and provider usage during execution by round, phase, task, activity and actual execution profile. Preserve profile aliases, resolved models and settings. Distinguish wall time, nested durations and approval waits.

Preserve accounting semantics: token subsets and nested durations cannot be added twice, and absent usage remains unknown. Cost estimates require a verified dated pricing or billing basis and remain distinct from billed amounts.

Link aggregates to retained observations. Later planning and architecture assessments use prior measurements to evaluate concrete improvement hypotheses against useful outcomes, reliability and quality. Measurement authorizes no quotas, automatic profile changes or weakened acceptance.

## Success measures

- Live totals reconcile with underlying observations without duplicate accounting.
- Missing usage and pricing remain visibly unknown.
- Failed work, repairs, waits and successful delivery remain represented.
- A later round uses measurements to choose or evaluate a useful improvement.

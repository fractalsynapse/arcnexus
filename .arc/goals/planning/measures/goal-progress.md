# Connect approved targets to strategies and verified progress

Preserve the proposed governance outcome for exact architecture review before runtime adoption. Quantitative goals identify measure, unit, approved target, calculation and comparable conditions. Changes to success or targets use the existing goal approval path; illustrative mockup values are not project targets.

Link strategy revisions and milestone tactics to approved goal revisions and intended measures. Keep assignments and observed values in run records, retaining measure definitions, versions, inputs, candidate or staging identities and observations.

Show baseline and latest verified progress separately from candidate measurements and delivery verdicts. A verified improvement may leave the standing target unmet. Changed measurement definitions require comparable baselines before trend interpretation.

## Success measures

- Missing evidence appears unavailable rather than zero.
- Candidate observations preserve the last verified staging value.
- Later runs recover strategy and measurement history to justify continuation, revision or no change.
- Only approved goal refinement changes the success target.

# Trace hosted Plan decisions through goal alignment

Let an operator follow a durable, readable chain from an objective's original
submission and complete clarification dialogue through the exact reviewed
proposal, human decision and resulting goal dispositions. Preserve objective
identity and explicit refinement history so later assessments can explain why
accepted direction changed.

Keep the selected project, Plan execution and captured guide revision consistent
across objective review, proposal details, approval and alignment views. A newer
execution or refreshed project revision must not silently replace the context
being reviewed.

Make the objective's relationship to existing and proposed goals explicit,
including retained, changed and removed goals and unresolved mappings. Distinguish
source alignment from observed implementation support. Keep approved, declined,
unanswered, unresolved and justified no-change outcomes distinguishable; none
alone implies delivery of proposed records.

Retain the exact proposal, relevant baseline, question and complete human answer
through reconnect and host restart. Approval applies only to the reviewed guide
bytes and relevant baseline. Changed proposal content or a changed relevant
baseline requires a reconciled, reviewable decision that retains its relationship
to the original objective and prior decision.

Link accepted records and their actual delivery disposition back to the exact
human decision through the existing journal and application lifecycle. Keep
execution observations outside standing goal aspirations.

## Success measures

- An operator can discover and open an objective and navigate to its complete
  clarification, exact proposed diff, recorded decision and resulting goal
  dispositions without reconstructing a conversation or inferring relationships
  from timestamps. Explicit refinements retain their predecessor relationships.
- Reconnect and host-restart scenarios preserve the same pending proposal,
  question and complete answer identities. A newer Plan execution or refreshed
  project revision cannot silently change the selected review context or cause
  duplicate questions or answers.
- Approved, declined, unanswered, unresolved and justified no-change scenarios
  remain distinguishable. Declining proposed edits preserves the original
  objective; an unanswered proposal remains pending and resumable.
- Changed-proposal and changed-baseline scenarios refuse approval reuse for
  different guide bytes or a different relevant baseline. The reconciled decision
  remains linked to the original objective and prior decision.
- Goal alignment identifies supporting captured sources separately from execution
  evidence and explains retained, changed, removed and unresolved dispositions.
  Accepted records remain traceable to the exact approving decision and actual
  delivery disposition, without treating approval as delivery.

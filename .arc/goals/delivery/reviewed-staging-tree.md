# Verify staging delivers the independently reviewed tree

Keep delivery pinned to the accepted tree and reviewed staging parent. Reconcile
observed issue-branch and pull-request state before uncertain effects. Preserve
check and review gates and the final observed merge identity.

**Success measures.** Delivery scenarios exercise resumed publication, reused
merges, and rejection of conflicting refs or a moved staging base. A successful
real delivery can identify the exact tree on staging and its single PR.

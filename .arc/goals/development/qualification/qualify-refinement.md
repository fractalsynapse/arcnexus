# Qualify useful continuous self-development

Demonstrate recurring Plan → Architect → Run through production composition and real inference. Each round assesses objectives, goals and architecture, then evaluates every captured approved goal in priority order. Preserve the captured inventory, completed outcomes and approval position across interruption.

Qualify three consecutive clean real rounds that collectively demonstrate an approved guide improvement, useful staging delivery and later use of prior findings. Justified no change and handled local repairs remain valid. Operator state repair, bypassed checks and uncontrolled aborts break qualification; an execution-path correction starts a fresh streak. Broader activation remains subject to qualification and exact authorized exceptions.

## Success measures

- Three consecutive production rounds retain every captured goal's disposition.
- Approval pauses resume the same round without replaying completed work.
- Later assessments visibly consume earlier findings.
- Reports retain approvals, repairs, runtime identities, checks and staging commits, distinguishing deterministic, native, hosted and live evidence.
- Failures and assistance remain visible rather than counted as clean completion.

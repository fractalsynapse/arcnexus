# Authorize and verify exact production releases

Preserve the proposed production lifecycle for exact contract review. Existing maintainer authority remains in force until an approved amendment explicitly enables trusted-host effects.

Bind specialist reviews, required checks and the maintainer decision to the exact candidate, production base, scope, tag and deliverables. Changed identities require revalidation and renewed approval. Holds and change requests retain candidate identity and reasoning.

After authorization, reconcile and record merge, tag and publication separately with immutable identities. Verify actual published deliverables before claiming delivery. Missing evidence remains a named hold. Failed post-release checks preserve released artifacts and create one attributable issue-linked incident; retries reuse it. Correction proceeds through staging and fresh production authorization.

## Success measures

- Specialist rejection blocks approval; changed candidates invalidate prior approval.
- Cancellation and uncertain effects reconcile provider facts without duplicate publication.
- Delivered status requires confirmed effects and passing required post-release checks.
- Failed checks retain released identities, complete evidence and one recoverable incident.

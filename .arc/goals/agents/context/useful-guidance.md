# Provide complete guidance that supports useful decisions

Give each activation complete selected purpose, role, procedure and standards, separating operating guidance, task input and observations by source. Make originating findings, current checks, acknowledged receipts and complete retained answers discoverable.

Keep other responsibilities discoverable through a source-linked index. Preserve complete reports and artifacts through existing reads and indexes without recursive copying, arbitrary truncation or another evidence store. Describe actual capabilities and expose missing mappings honestly.

Evaluate guidance through observed decisions and useful delivery as well as static validity. Realistic public fixtures distinguish required behavior from plausible shortcuts; approval of prose alone does not establish improved execution.

## Success measures

- The real harness receives complete selected guidance with captured source identity.
- Nested repairs retain originating findings and complete relevant answers.
- Missing operations remain visible capability gaps.
- Live evaluations connect changed guidance with decisions and delivery while retaining causal uncertainty.
